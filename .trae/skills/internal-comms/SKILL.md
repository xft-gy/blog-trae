# Internal Comms

This skill handles internal communications and related tasks.

## Usage

1. **Define communication needs**: Specify the internal communication requirements
2. **Set parameters**: Configure communication details and format
3. **Create communication**: Generate the internal communication
4. **Review content**: Examine the communication content
5. **Distribute**: Share the communication with relevant parties
